## Usage
1. Select a multileader, or start the command and select one.
2. Pick the leader lines to remove.
3. Press Enter to finish.

## Options
| Option | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Add leaders** (`A`) | Switches to adding leaders, as in [`AIMLEADEREDITADD`](AIMLEADEREDITADD-Command). |

## Notes
- The remaining leaders are previewed while you pick.
- If you remove every leader, the content stays on its own and the command reports that no leaders are left to remove.
- The command is also on the right-click menu when one multileader is selected.
- All changes are applied together when you press Enter.
