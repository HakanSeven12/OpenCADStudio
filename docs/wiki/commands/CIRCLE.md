## Usage
1. Run `CIRCLE`.
2. At "Specify center point:", pick the center, or type an option.
3. At "Specify radius or type value", pick a point on the circle or type the radius. Press Enter to use the radius shown in angle brackets.

## Options
| Option | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Diameter** (`D`) | At the radius prompt: asks for the diameter instead. |
| **2P** (`2P`) | Pick the two ends of a diameter. |
| **3P** (`3P`) | Pick three points on the circle. |
| **Ttr** (`TTR` or `T`) | Select two objects the circle must touch, then give the radius. |
| **Ttt** (`TTT`) | Select three objects the circle must touch. |

## Notes
- The last radius you used becomes the default for the next circle.
- When a tangent construction has more than one answer, the circle that best fits the points where you picked the objects is used.
- The circle is drawn in the current working plane.
- The ribbon drop-down offers Center, Radius; Center, Diameter; 2-Point; 3-Point; Tan, Tan, Radius; and Tan, Tan, Tan.
