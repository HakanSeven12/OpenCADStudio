## Usage
1. Type `COMMANDLINE`. The command line appears again if it was hidden.

## Notes
- The choice is kept for your next session.
- To hide it again, use [`COMMANDLINEHIDE`](COMMANDLINEHIDE-Command) or [`COMMANDLINETOGGLE`](COMMANDLINETOGGLE-Command).
