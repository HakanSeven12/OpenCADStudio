---
summary: Constrains a point to stay at the center of a circle or arc.
related: MPCONSTRAINT, OCCONSTRAINT, AUTOCONSTRAIN, DELCONSTRAINT, CONSTRAINTBAR
---

## Usage
1. Select the circle or arc and press Enter. If you selected it first, this step is skipped.
2. Specify the point: pick an endpoint, center or vertex of another object.
3. The point moves to the center and stays there.

## Notes
- Select exactly one object. With more than one, the command clears the selection and asks you to try again.
- If the pick does not land on a point, the command ends with a hint. Turn on an Endpoint or Center object snap and try again.
