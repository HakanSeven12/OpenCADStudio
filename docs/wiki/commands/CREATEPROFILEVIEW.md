---
summary: Creates a Civil 3D profile view of an alignment and its profiles.
related: CREATEPROFILE, CREATEALIGNMENT
---

## Usage
1. Select an alignment that has a profile.
2. Specify the lower left corner of the view.

## Notes
- The view shows every profile of the alignment, with stations from 200 before the alignment's start to 200 past its end and elevations around the profiles, at a vertical scale of 10.
- A drawing without Civil 3D data gets the profile view style it needs.
