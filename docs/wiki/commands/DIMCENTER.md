---
summary: Draws a simple cross of two lines at the center of a circle or an arc.
related: CENTERMARK, CENTERLINE, DIMDIAMETER, DIMRADIUS
---

## Usage
1. Select an arc or a circle.
2. Two lines are drawn through its center, one along each axis of the object's plane.

## Notes
- Each line is 0.4 times the radius long.
- The lines are ordinary lines; they do not follow the circle or arc when it changes. For an associative center mark use [`CENTERMARK`](CENTERMARK-Command).
