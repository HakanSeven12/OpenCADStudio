## Usage
1. Select a linear or aligned dimension, or type `R` to remove a jog.
2. Specify the jog location on the dimension line, or press Enter to place it at a default position.
3. The jog is added and the command ends.

## Options
| Option | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Remove** (`R`) | Asks you to select a dimension and removes its jog. |

## Notes
- Other dimension types are rejected with a message.
