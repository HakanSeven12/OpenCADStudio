---
summary: Shows or hides individual edges of 3D faces.
related: 3DFACE
---

## Usage
1. Click near an edge of a 3D face. The edge under the cursor is highlighted while you hover.
2. The edge switches between visible and invisible. Matching edges of neighboring 3D faces that lie on the same line switch with it.
3. Keep clicking edges, or type an option. Press Enter to finish.

## Options
| Option | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Display** (`D`) | Shows the invisible edges so you can pick them. Then choose **All** (`A`) to show the invisible edges of every 3D face, or **Select** (`S`, the default) to pick the faces whose invisible edges you want to see, and press Enter when done. You return to the edge prompt. |

## Notes
- Only 3D face objects are affected.
- Invisible edges are shown in a highlight color while the command runs.
