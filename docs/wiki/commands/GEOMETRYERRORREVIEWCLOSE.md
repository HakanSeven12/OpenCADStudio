summary: Ends the review mode of Geometry Cleanup.
related: GEOMETRYCLEANUPOPEN, GEOMETRYCLEANUPCLOSE, GEOMETRYCLEANUPZOOMPAN
---

## Usage
1. Run `GEOMETRYERRORREVIEWCLOSE`. The drawing shows all objects in their own colours again, and the review toolbar closes.

## Notes
- A pick of an error in the [`GEOMETRYCLEANUPOPEN`](GEOMETRYCLEANUPOPEN-Command) palette starts review mode.
- **Close** on the review toolbar, a new **Select Objects** and closing the palette also end it.
