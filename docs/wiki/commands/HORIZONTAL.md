`HORIZONTAL` runs the two-viewport configuration of [`VPORTS`](VPORTS-Command) (`VPORTS 2H`).

## Notes
- In Model space, the drawing area is split into two viewports, one above the other.
- On a layout, two viewports are created side by side and fill the printable area of the sheet.
