---
## Usage
1. At `Display hyperlink cursor, tooltip, and shortcut menu? [Yes/No] <Yes>:`, type `Y` or `N`.
2. Press Enter without an answer to keep the current setting.

## Options
| Option | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Yes** (`Y`) | Turns the hyperlink cursor, tooltip and shortcut menu on. |
| **No** (`N`) | Turns them off. |

## Notes
- The default shown in the prompt is the current setting.
- The setting is saved with your preferences, not in the drawing.
- You can also type the answer after the command, for example `HYPERLINKOPTIONS N`.
