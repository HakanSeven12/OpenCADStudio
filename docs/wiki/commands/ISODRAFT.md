## Usage
- Type `ISODRAFT` to switch isometric drafting on or off.
- Type `ISODRAFT ON` (or `1`) to turn it on, and `ISODRAFT OFF` (or `0`) to turn it off.

## Notes
- With isometric drafting on, the crosshairs and Ortho follow the two axes of the current isometric plane instead of the UCS X and Y axes. Use [`ISOPLANE`](ISOPLANE-Command) or F5 to change the plane.
- The setting is also in the drafting settings ([`DSETTINGS`](DSETTINGS-Command)) as **Enable isometric drafting**.
- The setting is saved with your preferences.
