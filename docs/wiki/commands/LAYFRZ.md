## Usage
1. Select objects. If objects are already selected, their layers are frozen at once.
2. Otherwise, at the `Select objects:` prompt, pick the objects and press Enter.
3. The layers of those objects are frozen.

## Notes
- Layer `0` is never frozen by this command.
- Frozen layers are hidden. To thaw every layer again, use [`LAYTHW`](LAYTHW-Command).
- The change can be undone in one step.
