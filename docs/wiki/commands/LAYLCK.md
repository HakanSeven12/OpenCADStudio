## Usage
1. Select objects. If objects are already selected, their layers are locked at once.
2. Otherwise, at the `Select objects:` prompt, pick the objects and press Enter.
3. The layers of those objects are locked.

## Notes
- Objects on a locked layer stay visible and can be snapped to, but they cannot be selected or edited. They are drawn faded.
- To unlock layers, use [`LAYULK`](LAYULK-Command).
- The change can be undone in one step.
