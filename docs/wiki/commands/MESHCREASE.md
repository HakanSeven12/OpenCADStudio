---
summary: Sharpens mesh edges so that smoothing keeps them.
related: MESHUNCREASE, MESHSMOOTHMORE
---

## Usage
1. Pick mesh edges or faces (a face creases all its edges), then press Enter.
2. Specify the crease value, or **Always**.

## Notes
- A crease value is the number of smoothness levels the edge stays sharp for; Always keeps it sharp at every level.
