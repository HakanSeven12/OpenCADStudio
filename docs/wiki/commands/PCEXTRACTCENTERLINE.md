---
summary: Draws a line along the axis of a cylindrical part of a point cloud, such as a pipe.
related: PCEXTRACTEDGE, PCEXTRACTCORNER, PCEXTRACTSECTION, POINTCLOUDATTACH
---

## Usage
1. Select a cylindrical segment: click on the cylindrical surface in the point cloud.

A line is drawn along the cylinder's axis, over the length of the cylinder.

## Notes
- Only structured (segmented) scans give cylinders. On an unstructured scan, or where no cylinder is found under the cursor, the prompt asks again.
