---
summary: Creates a flat surface from two corners, or from closed planar objects.
related: SURFNETWORK, SURFPATCH, REGION
---

## Usage
1. Pick the first corner, then the other corner: a rectangular surface is made in the current UCS plane.
2. Or press Enter at the first prompt and select closed planar objects (circles, closed polylines, ellipses, closed splines): each becomes a planar surface.

## Notes
- The surface lies in the plane of the UCS at the first corner.
- Objects that are not closed or not flat are skipped.
