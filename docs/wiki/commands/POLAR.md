---
summary: Turns polar tracking on or off.
related: ORTHO, DSETTINGS, OSNAP
---

Run `POLAR` to switch polar tracking on or off. It does the same as the Polar Tracking button on the status bar.

## Notes
- Turning polar tracking on turns Ortho mode off.
- You can run it in the middle of another command; the running command keeps going.
- The tracking angles are set on the Polar Tracking tab of [`DSETTINGS`](DSETTINGS-Command).
