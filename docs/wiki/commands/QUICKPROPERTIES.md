## Usage
1. Run the command, or click the Quick Properties button on the status bar, to switch the panel on or off.
2. Select objects. The panel appears near the cursor and lists their properties, which you can change there.

## Notes
- The panel hides while a command runs or while you type at the command line.
- Drag the panel by its title bar to move it.
- The system variable `QPMODE` also switches the panel on (`1` or `2`) or off (`0`). `QPLOCATION` `1` keeps the panel where it was last dragged instead of near the cursor.
- The setting is remembered between sessions.
