---
summary: Lengthens a surface past one of its edges.
related: SURFTRIM, SURFUNTRIM
---

## Usage
1. Pick the surface edges to extend, then press Enter.
2. Type the extension distance.
3. Press Enter, or choose Modes first.

## Notes
- Modes 0 (Extend) continues the surface itself; 1 (Stretch) continues it in the direction it leaves the edge.
