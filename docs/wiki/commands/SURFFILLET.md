---
summary: Rounds the meeting of two surfaces with a fillet surface.
related: SURFBLEND, FILLETEDGE, SURFTRIM
---

## Usage
1. Pick the first surface where the fillet should sit.
2. Pick the second surface.
3. Press Enter to accept, or set the Radius or Trim surface option first.

## Notes
- Surfaces that cross are filleted in the corner your two picks point to.
- With Trim surface on (the default) both surfaces are cut back to the fillet.
