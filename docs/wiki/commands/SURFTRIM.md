summary: Cuts away part of a surface along curves.
related: SURFUNTRIM, SURFEXTEND, PROJECTGEOMETRY
---

## Usage
1. Select the surfaces to trim, then press Enter.
2. Select the cutting curves, then press Enter.
3. Pick the area of a surface to remove.

## Notes
- Cutting curves that do not lie on the surface are projected onto it along the UCS Z axis.
