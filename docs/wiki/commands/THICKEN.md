---
summary: Turns surfaces into 3D solids by giving them a thickness.
related: CONVTOSURFACE, EXTRUDE, PRESSPULL
---

## Usage
1. Select the surfaces to thicken, then press Enter (skipped if you selected surfaces before starting).
2. Specify the thickness: type a distance, or pick two points.

## Notes
- Only surfaces are taken; other selected objects are ignored. With no surfaces, you see "No surfaces selected."
- The thickness you enter is offered as the default next time.
- A thickness of 0 does nothing.
- The command line reports how many solids were made and any surfaces that could not be thickened.
