`VERTICAL` runs the two-viewport configuration of [`VPORTS`](VPORTS-Command) (`VPORTS 2V`).

## Notes
- In Model space, the drawing area is split into two viewports, side by side.
- On a layout, two viewports are created one above the other and fill the printable area of the sheet.
