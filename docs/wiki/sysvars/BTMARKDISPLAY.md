---
summary: Shows value-set markers for the hovered or active parameter in the Block Editor.
type: On/Off (0/1)
saved: Program settings
default: 1 (On)
related: BEDIT, BESETTINGS
---

## Values
| Value | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 0 | Value-set markers are not shown |
| 1 | Value-set markers are shown |

## Notes
- The markers are only shown on screen. They are not grips and you cannot snap to them.
- The [`BESETTINGS`](BESETTINGS-Command) dialog sets this value too.
