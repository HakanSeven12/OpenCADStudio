---
summary: Shows or hides camera glyphs in the drawing.
type: On/Off (0/1)
saved: Drawing
default: 0 (Hidden)
---

## Values
| Value | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 0 | Camera glyphs are hidden |
| 1 | Camera glyphs are shown |

## Notes
- A glyph is drawn for each camera object and for each saved perspective view.
