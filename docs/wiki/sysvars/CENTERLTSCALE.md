---
summary: Sets the linetype scale of new center lines and center marks.
type: Real
saved: Drawing
default: 1.0
related: CENTERLTYPE, CENTERLAYER, CENTERLINE, CENTERMARK
---

## Values
Any positive number. Zero and negative values are refused.

## Notes
- Used by [`CENTERLINE`](CENTERLINE-Command) and [`CENTERMARK`](CENTERMARK-Command).
