---
summary: Sets how close two points must be for the drawing comparison to treat them as the same.
type: Real
saved: Program settings
default: 0.0001
related: COMPARECOLORADDED, COMPARECOLORMODIFIED, COMPARECOLORREMOVED, COMPARE
---

## Values
Any positive number, in drawing units. Zero and negative values are refused.

## Notes
- Used by [`COMPARE`](COMPARE-Command).
