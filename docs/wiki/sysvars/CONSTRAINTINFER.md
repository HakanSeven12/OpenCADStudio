---
summary: Controls whether geometric constraints are applied automatically while you draw and edit.
type: On/Off (0/1)
saved: Program settings
default: 0 (Off)
related: AUTOCONSTRAIN, GEOMCONSTRAINT, CONSTRAINTSETTINGS
---

## Values
| Value | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 0 | No constraints are added automatically. |
| 1 | Suitable geometric constraints are inferred and applied to the objects you create or change. |
