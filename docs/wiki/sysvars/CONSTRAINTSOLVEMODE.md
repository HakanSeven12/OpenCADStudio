---
summary: Controls whether objects keep their size when constraints are solved after an edit.
type: On/Off (0/1)
saved: Program settings
default: 1 (On)
related: GEOMCONSTRAINT, DIMCONSTRAINT, AUTOCONSTRAIN
---

## Values
| Value | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 0 | The solver does not try to keep the size of objects. |
| 1 | The solver tries to keep the existing size of objects and moves them instead. |
