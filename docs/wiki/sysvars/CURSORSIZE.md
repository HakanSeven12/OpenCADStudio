---
summary: Sets the length of the crosshair arms.
type: Integer
saved: Program settings
default: 5
related: CURSORTYPE, PICKBOX, OPTIONS
---

## Values
An integer from 1 to 100. 5 gives short arms around the cursor. Higher values make the arms longer; at 100 they reach across the whole drawing area.

## Notes
- Also set with the crosshair size slider in the [`OPTIONS`](OPTIONS-Command) dialog.
