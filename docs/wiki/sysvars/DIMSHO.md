---
summary: Controls whether dimensions update while you drag them.
type: On/Off (0/1)
saved: Drawing
default: 1 (On)
related: DIMASSOC
---

## Values
| Value | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 0 | Off. |
| 1 | On. |

## Notes
- Stored with the drawing; it does not change anything in Open CAD Studio yet.
- SETVAR also accepts ON/OFF and TRUE/FALSE.
