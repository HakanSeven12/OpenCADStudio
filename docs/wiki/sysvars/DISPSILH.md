---
summary: Controls whether silhouette edges of 3D solids are shown in wireframe and edge display modes.
type: On/Off (0/1)
saved: Drawing
default: 1 (On)
related: ISOLINES
---

## Values
| Value | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 0 | Silhouette edges are not drawn (unless the visual style or the hidden-line mode draws them). |
| 1 | Silhouette edges of curved solids are drawn in wireframe views and in views that show 3D edges. |

## Notes
- You can also set it in the Options dialog: "Show silhouette edges on solids".
- SETVAR also accepts ON/OFF and TRUE/FALSE.
