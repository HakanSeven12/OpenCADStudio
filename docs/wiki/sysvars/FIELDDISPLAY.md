---
summary: Controls whether fields are shown on a gray background.
type: On/Off (0/1)
saved: Program settings
default: 1 (On)
related: FIELDEVAL, FIELD, UPDATEFIELD
---

## Values
| Value | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 0 | Fields look like the rest of the text. |
| 1 | Fields are shown on a gray background. |

## Notes
- The background is shown on screen only.
- The setting applies to every open drawing; changing it redraws their fields at once.
- Fields in table cells follow it too.
