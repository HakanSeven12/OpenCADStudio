## Values
`FRAMES3` takes no value and does nothing when you enter it.

## Notes
- It is the "Frames vary" item of the Frames list on the Insert tab of the ribbon. The list shows it while [`FRAME`](FRAME-System-Variable) reads 3. You cannot pick it.
