---
summary: Tells whether the Geometry Cleanup palette is open.
type: On/Off (0/1) read-only
saved: Not saved
default: 0 (Closed)
related: GEOMETRYCLEANUPOPEN, GEOMETRYCLEANUPCLOSE
---

## Values
| Value | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 0 | The Geometry Cleanup palette is closed |
| 1 | The Geometry Cleanup palette is open |

## Notes
- [`GEOMETRYCLEANUPOPEN`](GEOMETRYCLEANUPOPEN-Command) and [`GEOMETRYCLEANUPCLOSE`](GEOMETRYCLEANUPCLOSE-Command) open and close the palette.
