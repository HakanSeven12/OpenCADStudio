---
summary: Sets the X and Y spacing of the grid.
type: Point (X,Y)
saved: Program settings
default: 10,10
related: GRIDMODE, GRID, DSETTINGS
---

## Values
Enter two positive numbers separated by a comma (for example `10,5`), or one positive number for both directions.

## Notes
- The Drafting Settings dialog ([`DSETTINGS`](DSETTINGS-Command)) sets the same spacing.
