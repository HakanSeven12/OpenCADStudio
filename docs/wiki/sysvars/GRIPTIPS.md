---
summary: Controls whether tips are shown when the cursor is over a grip of a dynamic block or an associative array.
type: On/Off (0/1)
saved: Program settings
default: 1 (On)
related: GRIPDYNCOLOR, BESETTINGS
---

## Values
| Value | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 0 | No grip tips. |
| 1 | A tip describes the grip under the cursor. |

## Notes
- You can also set it in the Block Editor Settings dialog ([`BESETTINGS`](BESETTINGS-Command)).
