---
summary: Controls how grips are shown when a whole group is selected.
type: Integer
saved: Program settings
default: 2 (Center grip and box)
related: GROUP, PICKSTYLE
---

## Values
| Value | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 0 | Every member of the group shows its own grips. |
| 1 | One grip at the center of the group. |
| 2 | One grip at the center of the group and a box around it. |

## Notes
- Grips already shown keep their look until you select again.
