---
summary: Controls whether new hatches are associative and follow their boundaries.
type: On/Off (0/1)
saved: Program settings
default: 1 (On)
related: HATCH, HPGAPTOL, HATCHLAYER
---

## Values
| Value | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 0 | New hatches are not linked to their boundaries. |
| 1 | New hatches update when their boundary objects change. |

## Notes
- You can also set it in the Options dialog: "New hatches follow their boundaries".
