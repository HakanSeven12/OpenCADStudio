---
summary: Controls whether the Hatch settings panel is shown while HATCH runs.
type: Integer
saved: Program settings
default: 2
related: HATCH, HPASSOC
---

## Values
| Value | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 0 | The panel is not shown. Use the settings option of [`HATCH`](HATCH-Command) to open it. |
| 1 | The panel is shown. |
| 2 | The panel is shown. |

## Notes
- 1 and 2 behave the same in Open CAD Studio.
- The panel floats at the top left of the drawing area.
