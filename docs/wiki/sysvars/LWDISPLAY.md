---
summary: Controls whether objects are drawn with their lineweights.
type: On/Off (0/1)
saved: Drawing
default: 0 (Off)
related: LWEIGHT
---

## Values
| Value | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 0 | All objects are drawn with thin lines. |
| 1 | Objects are drawn with their lineweights. |

## Notes
- The Show Lineweight button on the status bar switches it.
- Typed alone, it asks for `ON` or `OFF`. `LWDISPLAY ON` and `LWDISPLAY OFF` set it directly.
- Changing it can be undone.
