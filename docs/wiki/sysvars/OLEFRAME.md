---
summary: Controls whether the frames of OLE objects are shown and plotted.
type: Integer
saved: Drawing
default: 2 (shown but not plotted)
related: INSERTOBJ, OLEHIDE, OLEQUALITY, FRAME
---

## Values
| Value | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 0 | OLE frames are hidden. |
| 1 | OLE frames are shown and plotted. |
| 2 | OLE frames are shown but not plotted. |

## Notes
- A value outside 0 to 2 is refused.
