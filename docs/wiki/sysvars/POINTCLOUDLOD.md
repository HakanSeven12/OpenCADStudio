---
summary: Sets the level of detail of point clouds in tenths.
type: Integer
saved: Drawing
default: 10 (Full detail)
related: POINTCLOUDDENSITY, POINTCLOUDPOINTMAX
---

## Values
Accepted values are whole numbers from 1 to 10.

## Notes
- The number of points shown is scaled by this value divided by 10. With 5, half as many points are shown as with 10.
