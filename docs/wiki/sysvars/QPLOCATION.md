---
summary: Sets where the Quick Properties panel appears.
type: On/Off (0/1)
saved: Program settings
default: 0 (Follows the cursor)
related: QPMODE, QUICKPROPERTIES
---

## Values
| Value | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 0 | The panel appears near the cursor. |
| 1 | The panel stays where you last dragged it. |
