---
summary: Drawing setting for automatic regeneration of the drawing.
type: On/Off (0/1)
saved: Drawing
default: 1 (On)
related: REGEN, REGENALL
---

## Values
| Value | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 0 | Off. |
| 1 | On. |

## Notes
- Stored with the drawing; it does not change anything in Open CAD Studio yet. The display always updates on its own; [`REGEN`](REGEN-Command) and [`REGENALL`](REGENALL-Command) rebuild it on request.
