---
summary: Controls whether selection windows are filled with a see-through color.
type: On/Off (0/1)
saved: Program settings
default: 1 (On)
related: SELECTIONAREAOPACITY, WINDOWAREACOLOR, CROSSINGAREACOLOR
---

## Values
| Value | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 0 | Selection windows show only their outline. |
| 1 | Window and crossing selections are filled with a see-through color. |
