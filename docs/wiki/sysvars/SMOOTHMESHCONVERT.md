---
summary: Sets whether meshes converted to solids or surfaces are smooth or faceted, and whether flat faces are merged.
type: Integer
saved: Not saved
default: 0
related: CONVTOSOLID, CONVTOSURFACE
---

## Values
| Value | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 0 | Smooth, optimized: flat faces merged |
| 1 | Smooth, not optimized |
| 2 | Faceted, optimized |
| 3 | Faceted, not optimized |
