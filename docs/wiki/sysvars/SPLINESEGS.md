---
summary: Sets how many segments each span of a spline-fit polyline is drawn with.
type: Integer
saved: Drawing
default: 8
related: PEDIT, SPLFRAME, SPLINETYPE
---

## Values
Any whole number. A negative value is used as its positive size; 0 counts as 1.

## Notes
- Used when a 2D polyline is fitted as a Quadratic or Cubic spline from the Fit/Smooth row of the Properties panel, and when such a polyline is fitted again after an edit.
