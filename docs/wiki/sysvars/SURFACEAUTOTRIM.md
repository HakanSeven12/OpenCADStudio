---
summary: Sets whether PROJECTGEOMETRY trims a surface with the curves it projects onto it.
type: Integer
saved: Not saved
default: 0
related: PROJECTGEOMETRY, SURFTRIM
---

## Values
| Value | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 0 | Projected curves are drawn as new curves |
| 1 | A surface loses the area the projected curves enclose |

The Auto Trim button on the Surface tab switches it.
