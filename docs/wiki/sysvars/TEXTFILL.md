---
summary: Controls whether TrueType text is drawn filled or as outlines.
type: On/Off (0/1)
saved: Program settings
default: 1 (filled)
related: TEXTQLTY
---

## Values
| Value | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 0 | TrueType text is drawn as hollow outlines. |
| 1 | TrueType text is drawn filled. |

## Notes
- You can type 0/1 or OFF/ON. All open text is redrawn at once.
