---
summary: Turns the dynamic UCS on or off.
type: On/Off (0/1)
saved: Program settings
default: 1 (On)
related: UCS
---

## Values
| Value | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 0 | Off |
| 1 | On: when a command's first point is on a flat face of a solid, the UCS follows that face until the command ends |

## Notes
- The dynamic UCS button on the status bar toggles it.
