---
summary: Sets whether an orthographic view preset also sets the matching orthographic UCS.
type: On/Off (0/1)
saved: Program settings
default: 1 (On)
related: UCS, VIEW
---

## Values
| Value | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 0 | View presets leave the UCS unchanged |
| 1 | Choosing Top, Front, Left and the other orthographic presets also sets their UCS; Top sets the world UCS |

## Notes
- It does not apply while a block is being edited.
