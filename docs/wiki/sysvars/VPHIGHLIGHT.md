---
summary: Turns on or off the outline around the Model viewport under the cursor.
type: On/Off (0/1)
saved: Program settings
default: 1 (On)
related: VPHIGHLIGHTOPACITY
---

## Values
| Value | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 0 | No outline |
| 1 | The viewport under the cursor gets a faint outline when the Model view is split into several viewports |

## Notes
- It also accepts ON, OFF and TOGGLE.
- The Hover Outline button on the View ribbon tab toggles it.
- [`VPHIGHLIGHTOPACITY`](VPHIGHLIGHTOPACITY-System-Variable) sets how strong the outline is.
