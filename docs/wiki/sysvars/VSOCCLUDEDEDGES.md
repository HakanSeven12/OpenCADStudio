---
summary: Shows the edges of 3D solids that faces hide as dashed lines in the Hidden visual style.
type: Integer
saved: Program settings
default: 0
related: VSCURRENT, VSEDGECOLOR
---

- 0: hidden edges are not shown.
- 1: hidden edges are drawn dashed, in the edge color.

A visual style whose edge settings turn on obscured edges shows them too.

## Notes
- Applies to the Hidden visual style only. See [`VSCURRENT`](VSCURRENT-System-Variable).
