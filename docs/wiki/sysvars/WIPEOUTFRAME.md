---
summary: Sets whether the frames of wipeouts are shown and plotted.
type: Integer
saved: Drawing
default: 1 (Shown and plotted)
related: WIPEOUT, FRAME, IMAGEFRAME, XCLIPFRAME
---

## Values
| Value | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 0 | Frames are hidden and not plotted |
| 1 | Frames are shown and plotted |
| 2 | Frames are shown but not plotted |

## Notes
- The Frames option of [`WIPEOUT`](WIPEOUT-Command) sets it too.
- [`FRAME`](FRAME-System-Variable) sets this and the other frame variables together.
