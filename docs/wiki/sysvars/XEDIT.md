---
summary: Sets whether other drawings may edit this drawing in place when it is used as an external reference.
type: On/Off (0/1)
saved: Drawing
default: 1 (On)
related: REFEDIT
---

## Values
| Value | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 0 | In-place editing not allowed |
| 1 | In-place editing allowed |

## Notes
- Stored with the drawing; it does not change anything in Open CAD Studio yet.
